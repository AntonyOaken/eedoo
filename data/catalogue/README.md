# Overture pilot — 2026-09-23.1

Read-only comparison of fixed central areas with the current OSM catalogue. Records are review candidates, not verified new businesses. The report is not imported into the app or included in its frontend bundle. High confidence is the provider's existence estimate, not confirmation of current schedules or identity.

Provenance and upstream licenses are preserved in each record's `sources`: CDLA-Permissive-2.0, Apache-2.0 and CC0-1.0 appear in this sample. Before incorporating or distributing records, follow source-specific attribution and licensing requirements: https://docs.overturemaps.org/attribution/ .

The report is intentionally broader than our supported categories; `reviewCandidates` filters relevant categories, confidence >= 0.8, and excludes records marked permanently_closed. Matching uses name similarity and coordinates and can miss alternative-language names or branches. Review individually before adding, replacing or removing anything.
